## Design Context

### Users
Teens and young adults. Use context: discovering and playing webcam mini-games, often in group settings or sharing clips with friends on TikTok/Instagram. Traffic largely arrives from social sharing. Decision to play happens in seconds — the interface must grab before they think.

### Brand Personality
Fun, Viral, Cool. Energy of a Japanese arcade hall's attract screen — loud, physical, self-confident. Slightly chaotic and irreverent. Think: the feeling of dropping a token into a cabinet, not opening an app.

Reference: 67speed.com/duel — minimal but intentional, custom variable font, physical feel.

Anti-references: Google products, generic SaaS dashboards, purple-to-blue gradient "gaming aesthetic," AI-generated glassmorphism interfaces.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark theme (locked). Hyperactive arcade cabinet energy. Big condensed Teko display type for all headings and game titles. Barlow for body. Vivid arcade orange accent (`oklch(0.82 0.22 50)`) — warm, urgent, unlike any typical game site. Dot-grid background texture on hero for CRT/screen effect. Fast, snappy Framer Motion (no floaty easing).

### Design Principles
1. **Typography does the heavy lifting** — oversized condensed type creates drama; no hero images needed
2. **Color is rare and loud** — one accent color, used sparingly for maximum impact
3. **Each game has its own identity** — gradient variety per card within a coherent system
4. **Fast and physical** — entrances snap, hover states are immediate, nothing drifts
5. **Never look generated** — no gradient text, no border-left stripes, no glassmorphism cards
